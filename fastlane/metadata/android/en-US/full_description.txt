The World Labour Values Database (WLVD) provides estimates of labour values, prices of production and related indicators computed from national input-output tables.

This app is a lightweight native wrapper around the official WLVD panel (worldlabourvalues.org):

- Explore the map, country, indicators, trade and download tabs
- Download data spreadsheets (XLSX/CSV) to a local folder with quick offline access
- Pull-to-refresh, share the current page and open external links in the system browser
- Remembers your language and last visited tab

The panel behaviour lives on the web; the wrapper adds native conveniences on top of it. All source code is available under GPL-3.0.
